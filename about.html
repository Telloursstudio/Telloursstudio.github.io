A studio built around story, and around people.

Founded in 2026, Tell Ours Studio is a film and media production company dedicated to telling stories with clarity and staying power. We produce cinematic documentaries, branded films, commercial campaigns, event films, photography, and digital media for nonprofits, universities, businesses, foundations, and mission-driven organizations.

We believe storytelling can reshape culture. Every project is approached with the care of a documentary — whether we're filming a national campaign, covering a community event, or creating a short film. Our work blends cinematic visuals with emotional honesty to help organizations connect with people, not just audiences.

Tell Ours Studio is also an institution built around opportunity. We recruit, train, and mentor emerging creatives who may have never stepped onto a professional film set before. Talent exists everywhere; access to practice and trusted guidance does not. By investing in people while producing exacting work, we're building a studio where films and filmmakers can develop together.
