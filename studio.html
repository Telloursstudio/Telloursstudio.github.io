04The lounge

The
Sanctuary.

Every wall is a memory. A mural by New Haven artist Rouse wraps the room — Spike, Ava, Coogler; Boyz n the Hood, Menace, Shottas, Paid in Full, The Great Debaters. The faces we studied. The films that raised us.

It's where we decompress between takes, screen rough cuts, and remember why we picked up a camera in the first place. You walk in and the room reminds you: this lineage is the assignment.
